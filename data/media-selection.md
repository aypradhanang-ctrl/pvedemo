# Event photography selections

I selected these photographs from the supplied Google Drive previews. Each set has one cover and an ordered gallery. The 25 selected originals have now been downloaded, converted to WebP and implemented as five website albums. Covers were visually checked after export; gallery dimensions and file delivery were checked automatically.

I have kept these six supplied events separate from the seven historical spreadsheet records. Photos must not be assigned to a different event just to fill a gap.

## Export and placement

- Export covers at approximately 2400 pixels wide in WebP, with a separate mobile crop where needed. Keep the uncropped master.
- Export gallery photographs at approximately 1600 pixels on the long edge; preserve portrait/landscape proportions.
- Confirm full-resolution focus, colour and crop before treating these preview-based selections as publish-ready.
- Keep existing photographer credits and confirm credit requirements before publication. Do not remove watermarks.
- Do not serve Drive preview URLs or RAW files as website image assets.
- The implementation uses `data/gallery-assets.json` and `public/images/gallery/`. The original source files remain outside the repository; `scripts/import-event-photos.mjs` records the import process.
